K-Counter is a website prototype for demonstration purposes, not a live ordering or payment service.

Using the prototype

You may browse the pages and use the interactive forms to preview the intended ordering experience. Please do not rely on the prototype to place a real order or make a purchase.

Orders and payment

Submitting an order form only displays a sample confirmation in your browser. It does not send an order to a restaurant. Payment choices are for demonstration only; no payment is collected or processed.

Menu and pricing

Menu items, descriptions, images, availability, and prices are examples and may be incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated. Confirm current details with the restaurant or ordering service before making a purchase.

Availability and changes

The prototype is provided as-is for demonstration and may be changed or unavailable at any time. No production service, order fulfilment, or customer response is promised through this site.

Questions

For more information about this demonstration, visit Customer support. These sample terms should be reviewed and adapted before using K-Counter as a real commercial service.
