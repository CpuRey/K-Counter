Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy describes the current K-Counter website prototype. It is not a substitute for a reviewed privacy notice for a production service.

Information entered in forms

The ordering pages may ask for your name, address, contact number, and selected menu items. The support page may ask for your name, email address, and message. In this prototype, page scripts use these values only in your browser to demonstrate a summary or preview. The site has no built-in service that sends or stores these form entries.

Please do not enter real personal or payment information into this demonstration.

Payments and orders

The site does not submit orders to restaurants or process digital payments. Selecting a payment method only changes the sample confirmation shown in your browser.

Cookies and tracking

The K-Counter prototype contains no code that sets cookies or performs analytics tracking. The web host or browser you use may independently process technical data under its own policies.

Changes and questions

This notice may need to change if the prototype gains a server, analytics, or other data-handling features. For questions about the demo, visit Customer support.
